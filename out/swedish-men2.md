Sweden has internationally been pioneers in its strong efforts to promote development and

Starting point and purpose
Within the scope of work on the government's national equality policy goals, the Equality Authority should promote preventative measures against violence against women by men and support the implementation of
National strategy to prevent and combat violence against women. The strategy emphasizes two factors; the importance of preventive measures, with a focus on perpetrators and masculinity norms, as well as men's
participation and responsibility. 6
This is an area where there is limited knowledge. The authority
has therefore carried out a knowledge inventory, which consists of two parts. Firstly, an investigation of the current
national and international research, as well as a study based on interviews. The purpose was to investigate which factors
can strengthen the conditions for boys and men to actively choose to participate and take responsibility
promote equality and combat violence against women.

Preventive violence methods
In Sweden, the government adopted a national violence prevention program in 2022 that, with a focus on equality, focuses on perpetrators of violence and the need to combat harmful masculine norms that promote violence.16
The research shows that violence prevention measures
focuses on boys and men as a group has a greater effect than those that specifically target girls and women
because men are the perpetrators of the majority of violence. It shows
even though violence prevention initiatives that work with
masculinity norms that promote violence and include
man as part of the solution gives better results. 17
A research overview of the work on violence prevention in
Sweden shows that there are a number of violence prevention
methods and programs, where there are methods that are seen
a promising and with development potential. At the same time
are most programs not structured and few have been evaluated.18 There are international studies that show
suitable programs for boys and men can lead to significant changes in attitudes and behavior regarding
men's violence against women, men's violence against men as well as men's
